Quantitative Methods

Future value (discrete compounding)
\[FV = PV\,(1+r)^{N}\]
PV present value; r periodic rate; N number of periods — Growing a single cash flow forward
Present value of an ordinary annuity
\[PV = A\,\frac{1-(1+r)^{-N}}{r}\]
A level payment at end of each period; r periodic rate; N periods — Loan payments, bond coupons, retirement income
Present value of a perpetuity
\[PV = \frac{A}{r}\]
A constant payment per period; r discount rate — Preferred stock, consols
Effective annual rate
\[EAR = \left(1+\frac{r_s}{m}\right)^{m}-1\]
r_s stated annual rate; m compounding periods per year — Comparing rates with different compounding frequencies
Continuous compounding
\[FV = PV\,e^{r_s N}\]
r_s stated annual rate; N years — Continuously compounded returns and growth
Holding period return
\[HPR = \frac{P_1 - P_0 + D_1}{P_0}\]
P_0 beginning price; P_1 ending price; D_1 income received — Single-period return including income
Geometric mean return
\[\bar R_G = \left[\prod_{t=1}^{T}(1+R_t)\right]^{1/T}-1\]
R_t return in period t; T number of periods — Compound average growth rate over multiple periods
Sample variance
\[s^2 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_i-\bar X)^2}{n-1}\]
X_i observations; \bar X sample mean; n sample size — Dispersion of a sample
Coefficient of variation
\[CV = \frac{s}{\bar X}\]
s sample standard deviation; \bar X sample mean — Risk per unit of return
Sharpe ratio
\[S_p = \frac{\bar R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p}\]
\bar R_p portfolio mean return; R_f risk-free rate; \sigma_p portfolio standard deviation — Risk-adjusted performance
Sample correlation
\[r_{XY} = \frac{s_{XY}}{s_X\, s_Y}\]
s_{XY} sample covariance; s_X, s_Y sample standard deviations — Strength of linear relationship
Bayes' formula
\[P(A\mid B) = \frac{P(B\mid A)\,P(A)}{P(B)}\]
P(A) prior; P(B|A) likelihood; P(B) unconditional probability — Updating probabilities with new information
z-statistic / standardization
\[z = \frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\]
X value; \mu mean; \sigma standard deviation — Standard normal probabilities
t-statistic for a mean
\[t = \frac{\bar X-\mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}}\]
\bar X sample mean; \mu_0 hypothesized mean; s sample std dev; n sample size (df = n-1) — Hypothesis test of a population mean
Simple linear regression slope
\[\hat b_1 = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}{\operatorname{Var}(X)}\]
Cov(X,Y) covariance; Var(X) variance of the independent variable — Estimating the slope coefficient
Coefficient of determination (simple regression)
\[R^2 = \frac{SSR}{SST} = 1-\frac{SSE}{SST}\]
SSR regression sum of squares; SSE sum of squared errors; SST total sum of squares — Share of variation explained

Economics

Price elasticity of demand
\[E_P = \frac{\%\Delta Q_d}{\%\Delta P}\]
Q_d quantity demanded; P price — Responsiveness of demand to price
Covered interest rate parity (forward rate)
\[F_{f/d} = S_{f/d}\,\frac{1+i_f\left(\frac{\text{days}}{360}\right)}{1+i_d\left(\frac{\text{days}}{360}\right)}\]
S spot rate (price currency f per base currency d); i_f, i_d money-market rates — Pricing currency forwards

Financial Statement Analysis

Current ratio
\[\text{Current ratio} = \frac{\text{Current assets}}{\text{Current liabilities}}\]
— — Short-term liquidity
Cash conversion cycle
\[CCC = DOH + DSO - \text{Days of payables}\]
DOH days of inventory on hand; DSO days of sales outstanding — Working-capital efficiency
DuPont (three-step) ROE
\[ROE = \frac{NI}{\text{Sales}}\times\frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Assets}}\times\frac{\text{Assets}}{\text{Equity}}\]
NI net income; average assets and equity — Decomposing return on equity
Basic EPS
\[EPS = \frac{NI - \text{Preferred dividends}}{\text{Weighted avg. common shares}}\]
NI net income — Earnings per common share
Interest coverage
\[\text{Interest coverage} = \frac{EBIT}{\text{Interest payments}}\]
EBIT earnings before interest and taxes — Solvency

Corporate Issuers

Weighted average cost of capital
\[WACC = w_d\,r_d\,(1-t) + w_p\,r_p + w_e\,r_e\]
w weights (market values); r_d before-tax cost of debt; t marginal tax rate; r_p cost of preferred; r_e cost of equity — Hurdle rate for average-risk projects
Net present value
\[NPV = \sum_{t=0}^{N}\frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t}\]
CF_t cash flow at t (CF_0 usually negative); r required return — Capital budgeting accept/reject
Degree of operating leverage
\[DOL = \frac{\%\Delta EBIT}{\%\Delta \text{Sales}} = \frac{Q(P-V)}{Q(P-V)-F}\]
Q units; P price; V variable cost per unit; F fixed operating costs — Operating risk
Degree of financial leverage
\[DFL = \frac{\%\Delta NI}{\%\Delta EBIT} = \frac{Q(P-V)-F}{Q(P-V)-F-C}\]
C fixed financial costs (interest) — Financial risk
CAPM cost of equity
\[r_e = R_f + \beta\,[E(R_M)-R_f]\]
R_f risk-free rate; \beta equity beta; E(R_M) expected market return — Required return on equity

Equity Investments

Gordon (constant) growth model
\[V_0 = \frac{D_1}{r-g}\]
D_1 next dividend; r required return; g constant growth rate (g < r) — Valuing mature dividend payers
Justified forward P/E
\[\frac{P_0}{E_1} = \frac{D_1/E_1}{r-g}\]
D_1/E_1 payout ratio; r required return; g growth — Relative valuation from fundamentals
Preferred stock value
\[V_0 = \frac{D_p}{r_p}\]
D_p fixed preferred dividend; r_p required return — Non-callable perpetual preferred shares
Margin call price (long)
\[P_{call} = P_0\,\frac{1-IM}{1-MM}\]
P_0 purchase price; IM initial margin; MM maintenance margin — Leveraged long position

Fixed Income

Bond price from yield
\[P = \sum_{t=1}^{N}\frac{PMT}{(1+r)^t} + \frac{FV}{(1+r)^N}\]
PMT coupon per period; FV par value; r market discount rate per period; N periods — Pricing option-free bonds
Modified duration
\[\text{ModDur} = \frac{\text{MacDur}}{1+r}\]
MacDur Macaulay duration; r yield per period — Price sensitivity to yield
Percentage price change (duration + convexity)
\[\%\Delta P \approx -\text{AnnModDur}\,\Delta y + \tfrac12\,\text{AnnConvexity}\,(\Delta y)^2\]
\Delta y change in annual yield (decimal) — Estimating price change for a yield shift
Money duration and PVBP
\[\text{MoneyDur} = \text{AnnModDur}\times P^{\text{full}}, \quad PVBP = \frac{PV_- - PV_+}{2}\]
P^full full price; PV_- / PV_+ prices for 1 bp down / up yield — Dollar interest-rate exposure
Forward rate from spot rates
\[(1+S_{A+B})^{A+B} = (1+S_A)^A\,(1+{}_AF_B)^B\]
S spot rates; {}_AF_B forward rate starting at A for B periods — Implied forward rates / bootstrapping

Derivatives

Forward price (no income)
\[F_0(T) = S_0\,(1+r)^T\]
S_0 spot price; r risk-free rate; T years — No-arbitrage forward pricing
Put–call parity
\[S_0 + p_0 = c_0 + \frac{X}{(1+r)^T}\]
S_0 spot; p_0, c_0 European put/call; X strike; r risk-free; T years — Relating European put and call prices
One-period binomial risk-neutral probability
\[\pi = \frac{1+r-d}{u-d}\]
u, d up/down factors; r risk-free rate per period — Binomial option pricing

Portfolio Management

Two-asset portfolio variance
\[\sigma_p^2 = w_1^2\sigma_1^2 + w_2^2\sigma_2^2 + 2w_1w_2\rho_{12}\sigma_1\sigma_2\]
w weights; \sigma std devs; \rho_{12} correlation — Diversification effect
Beta
\[\beta_i = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_m)}{\sigma_m^2} = \rho_{i,m}\frac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_m}\]
R_i asset return; R_m market return — Systematic risk
Treynor ratio and Jensen's alpha
\[T_p = \frac{R_p-R_f}{\beta_p},\quad \alpha_p = R_p - [R_f + \beta_p(R_m-R_f)]\]
R_p portfolio return; \beta_p portfolio beta — Performance vs systematic risk

Alternative Investments

Hedge fund 2-and-20 fee (simplified)
\[\text{Fee} = 0.02\,AUM + 0.20\,\max(0,\ \text{Profit}-\text{Hurdle})\]
AUM assets under management; hurdle/high-water mark terms vary by fund — Net-of-fee return calculations

Quantitative Methods

Adjusted R-squared
\[\bar R^2 = 1-\left[\frac{n-1}{n-k-1}\right](1-R^2)\]
n observations; k independent variables — Comparing multiple regression models
F-statistic (regression)
\[F = \frac{SSR/k}{SSE/(n-k-1)}\]
SSR regression SS; SSE error SS; k slopes; n observations — Joint significance of slope coefficients
AR(1) mean-reverting level
\[\hat x_t = \frac{b_0}{1-b_1}\]
b_0, b_1 AR(1) coefficients (|b_1| < 1) — Time-series forecasting

Equities

Free cash flow to the firm
\[FCFF = NI + NCC + Int(1-t) - FCInv - WCInv\]
NCC non-cash charges; Int interest expense; FCInv fixed capital investment; WCInv working capital investment — FCFF valuation
Free cash flow to equity
\[FCFE = FCFF - Int(1-t) + \text{Net borrowing}\]
— — FCFE valuation
Sustainable growth rate
\[g = b \times ROE\]
b earnings retention ratio — Long-run growth input for DDM
Residual income
\[RI_t = E_t - r\,B_{t-1}\]
E_t earnings; r cost of equity; B_{t-1} beginning book value — Residual income valuation
Residual income model
\[V_0 = B_0 + \sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{E_t - rB_{t-1}}{(1+r)^t}\]
B_0 current book value per share — Valuing firms with no/irregular dividends

Fixed Income

Binomial interest-rate tree node value
\[V = \frac{0.5\,(V_H + C) + 0.5\,(V_L + C)}{1+i}\]
V_H, V_L values at next nodes; C coupon; i one-period forward rate at the node — Valuing bonds with embedded options
Value of callable / putable bond
\[V_{call} = V_{straight} - V_{call\ option},\quad V_{put} = V_{straight} + V_{put\ option}\]
— — Embedded option valuation
Credit valuation adjustment
\[CVA = \sum_{t}\text{LGD}_t \times \text{POD}_t \times DF_t\]
LGD loss given default; POD probability of default; DF discount factor — Valuing risky bonds

Derivatives and Risk Management

Forward with carry benefits/costs
\[F_0(T) = (S_0 - PV_0(I) + PV_0(C))(1+r)^T\]
PV_0(I) PV of income; PV_0(C) PV of carry costs — Forwards on income-paying assets
Black–Scholes–Merton call price
\[c_0 = S_0 N(d_1) - e^{-rT}X\,N(d_2),\ d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0/X)+(r+\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T},\ d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt T\]
N(·) standard normal CDF; \sigma volatility; r continuously compounded rate — European option pricing
Swap fixed rate
\[s_N = \frac{1 - PV_N}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} PV_i}\]
PV_i discount factor for period i — Pricing plain-vanilla interest rate swaps

Portfolio Construction

Arbitrage pricing theory
\[E(R_p) = R_F + \lambda_1\beta_{p,1} + \dots + \lambda_K\beta_{p,K}\]
\lambda_j factor risk premium; \beta_{p,j} factor sensitivity — Multifactor expected return
Parametric VaR (single asset)
\[VaR = (z_\alpha\,\sigma - \mu)\,V\]
z_\alpha standard normal quantile; \mu, \sigma period mean/std of return; V position value — Downside risk measurement

Foundations of Risk Management

CAPM expected return
\[E(R_i) = R_F + \beta_i\,[E(R_M)-R_F]\]
\beta_i asset beta — Required return / performance evaluation
Information ratio
\[IR = \frac{R_P - R_B}{\sigma(R_P-R_B)}\]
R_B benchmark return; denominator tracking error — Active-management skill
Sortino ratio
\[\text{Sortino} = \frac{R_P - R_{min}}{\sigma_{downside}}\]
R_min minimum acceptable return; downside deviation — Downside-risk-adjusted performance

Quantitative Analysis

Variance of a sum
\[\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY) = a^2\operatorname{Var}(X) + b^2\operatorname{Var}(Y) + 2ab\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)\]
a, b constants — Portfolio and aggregation math
OLS slope and intercept
\[\hat\beta = \frac{\sum (X_i-\bar X)(Y_i-\bar Y)}{\sum (X_i-\bar X)^2},\quad \hat\alpha = \bar Y - \hat\beta\bar X\]
— — Linear regression estimation
EWMA volatility
\[\sigma_t^2 = \lambda\,\sigma_{t-1}^2 + (1-\lambda)\,r_{t-1}^2\]
\lambda decay factor (e.g. 0.94 in RiskMetrics) — Volatility forecasting
GARCH(1,1)
\[\sigma_t^2 = \omega + \alpha\,r_{t-1}^2 + \beta\,\sigma_{t-1}^2,\quad \sigma_L^2 = \frac{\omega}{1-\alpha-\beta}\]
\omega constant; \alpha, \beta weights (\alpha+\beta < 1); \sigma_L long-run variance — Volatility forecasting with mean reversion

Financial Markets and Products

Minimum-variance hedge ratio
\[h^* = \rho\,\frac{\sigma_S}{\sigma_F}\]
\rho correlation of spot and futures changes; \sigma_S, \sigma_F std devs — Futures hedging
Optimal number of futures contracts
\[N^* = h^*\,\frac{Q_A}{Q_F}\]
Q_A size of position hedged; Q_F size of one contract — Futures hedging
Equity beta hedge
\[N^* = (\beta^* - \beta)\,\frac{P}{F}\]
\beta target beta; P portfolio value; F value of one futures contract — Changing portfolio beta with index futures
Futures price with storage cost and yield
\[F_0 = S_0\,e^{(r+u-q)T}\]
u storage cost rate; q income/convenience yield rate (continuous) — Commodity and asset futures pricing
Interest rate parity (continuous)
\[F_0 = S_0\,e^{(r-r_f)T}\]
r domestic rate; r_f foreign rate (continuous) — FX forward pricing

Valuation and Risk Models

Parametric VaR
\[VaR_\alpha = -\mu + z_\alpha\,\sigma\]
z_\alpha standard normal quantile (e.g. 1.645 at 95%); \mu, \sigma of P&L/returns — Market risk measurement
Expected shortfall (normal)
\[ES_\alpha = -\mu + \sigma\,\frac{\phi(z_\alpha)}{1-\alpha}\]
\phi standard normal pdf; \alpha confidence level — Tail risk beyond VaR
Expected loss (credit)
\[EL = PD \times LGD \times EAD\]
PD probability of default; LGD loss given default; EAD exposure at default — Credit risk
Option delta (BSM, non-dividend call/put)
\[\Delta_c = N(d_1),\quad \Delta_p = N(d_1) - 1\]
N(d_1) from BSM — Delta hedging
Effective duration and convexity
\[D_{eff} = \frac{V_- - V_+}{2V_0\,\Delta y},\quad C_{eff} = \frac{V_- + V_+ - 2V_0}{V_0\,(\Delta y)^2}\]
V_-/V_+ values for yield down/up by \Delta y — Interest rate risk (incl. embedded options)
